Operations Manager — Northwind Software (Remote)

Fictional employer note for this demo: Northwind Software makes Compass,
a support-queue product that shows team leads where routing delays begin.
Northwind says its design priority is helping leads correct queue problems
before customers need to follow up.

We are looking for an Operations Manager to run our 10-person customer support team.

Responsibilities
- Own support KPIs: first response time, resolution time, CSAT
- Hire, train and coach support agents
- Design and improve support processes and tooling
- Report weekly to the Head of Customer Experience

Requirements
- 3+ years in customer support, 1+ year leading a team
- Experience with a helpdesk tool (Zendesk, Freshdesk or similar)
- Strong written English

Application questions
1. Why do you want to work at Northwind? (150 words)
2. Describe a process you improved and the result. (200 words)
